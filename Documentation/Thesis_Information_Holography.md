## 0. The one-paragraph thesis

For reasoning tasks generated by rules local on a low-dimensional substrate, we posit an **operational area law**: the minimal information that must cross any cut of a solver — spatial cuts between grid regions, and scale cuts between RG levels — is set by the task's rule, is boundary-scaling for most natural rule families, and is *measurable*. A learner's memorization is precisely its **excess flux** above this floor. This is not an AdS/CFT duality claim; it is the identification of a single quantity — cut-crossing information — that five independent literatures bound (holographic min-cut/max-flow, classical MFMC, communication complexity, tensor-network expressivity, information-theoretic generalization bounds), instrumented for the first time in a trained solver on a controlled reasoning corpus (ARC).

## 1. The chain, adjudicated link by link

Chain: **RT/bit-threads ⇄ max-flow/min-cut ⇄ information complexity across cuts ⇄ network min-cut expressivity ⇄ measured variational flux.**

| Link | Statement | Rigor class | Source / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | In random tensor networks at large bond dimension, entanglement entropy of any boundary region = minimal cut (RT exactly), derived via an exact Ising-model mapping; entanglement-wedge/QEC structure included | **Solvable model (exact in a limit)** | Hayden–Nezami–Qi–Thomas–Walter–Yang, arXiv:1601.01694. *Known correction:* bulk entanglement displaces the surface and adds bulk entropy — pure min-cut fails once bulk matter matters |
| L2 | RT can be rewritten with no minimal surfaces: entropy = **max flux of a divergenceless, norm-bounded flow** ("bit threads"), via Riemannian max-flow/min-cut | **Theorem** (conditional on RT) | Freedman–Headrick, arXiv:1604.00354 |
| L3 | Quantum max-flow = quantum min-cut is **FALSE in general** tensor networks (explicit counterexamples), but **TRUE when all edge capacities are powers of a fixed integer d** — the uniform-bond-dimension regime | **Theorem + counterexample** | Cui et al. (quantum MFMC). *Design consequence:* our architecture uses uniform d per scale (inside the validity regime), and our ledger is **classical** Shannon information through explicit channels, where unconditional classical MFMC applies. The AI-side object deliberately lives where the stronger theorem holds |
| L4 | A ConvAC's representable correlation (separation rank) across an input partition is upper-bounded by the min edge-cut of its computation graph, channel numbers as capacities; equivalence to TN/wavefunctions is exact | **Theorem — scope-limited** | Levine–Yakira–Cohen–Shashua, arXiv:1704.01552. Proven **only for ConvACs** (linear + product pooling); ReLU/max-pool nets: empirical support only. *Our position:* we do not rely on the expressivity theorem for GELU nets — we **construct** the priced channels explicitly, so the measured quantity is exact by construction |
| L5 | Per-cut information flow, measured in a trained solver and tied to generalization vs memorization on a reasoning corpus | **Open — our contribution** | Nearest prior art (see §4) measures depth-wise information in NLG transformers; no spatial/scale cuts, no enforced channels, no ARC |

**Two classical anchors replace the "duality" (this is the key v0.1 refinement):**

- **Spatial cuts → communication complexity.** For a bipartition (A, Ā) of the grid, the information a solver must move across the cut to compute the rule is lower-bounded by the (information) communication complexity of the induced two-party function — Braverman–Rao information complexity is the exact classical formalism. *[Rigor: established field; our application to ARC rules is new. Verification pass pending on the specific theorem forms we cite.]*
- **Scale cuts → successive refinement.** Our per-scale stream flux {I_s} is a **successive-refinement rate profile**: I_s = information about the output that cannot be reconstructed from representations coarser than scale s. Rate-distortion/successive-refinement theory (Equitz–Cover lineage) is the right formal language; "area law" = geometric decay of the profile for local rules, "volume law" = flat profiles (identity/texture transport). **Honest caveat, made explicit:** the kept channel also crosses scale cuts; Σ I_s prices the *scale-bypass* (skip) information, not total cut flow — which is exactly the quantity the abstraction question cares about, and we say so rather than overclaiming. (A 2025 solvable-model result on bottleneck+skip linear autoencoders independently flags skip-paths as the confound for naive single-bottleneck measurements — arXiv:2505.24668 — which our all-streams-priced design addresses by construction.)

## 2. The formal centerpiece (candidate proposition, to prove for the paper)

**Definition (task flux floor).** For task rule R and cut family 𝒞 (spatial bipartitions; scale cuts), define Φ_R(c) = the infimum, over exact solvers factorizing through cut c with an explicit channel, of the Shannon information crossing c. 

**Proposition (to be proven).** (i) *Lower bound:* Φ_R(c) ≥ IC_R(c), the information complexity of the two-party function induced by R at cut c (data-processing across the cut). (ii) *Achievability in our class:* the QHRRN-2 family attains Φ_R(c) up to O(1) overhead for rules expressible in its program class, by opening only the required gates. (iii) *Measurement:* the β→0⁺ limit of the accuracy-constrained flux frontier estimates Φ_R empirically.

Sketch obligations: (i) is a standard data-processing/communication-complexity argument (adversarial input distributions realize the bound); (ii) is constructive per rule family (identity, shift, recolor worked examples — the CI-gate tasks double as the worked examples); (iii) needs the estimator's bias characterized (VIB gives an upper bound on I; the frontier is an upper envelope — so empirical spectra are certified *upper* bounds on floors, and lower bounds come from (i) analytically. The two-sided squeeze is the falsifiable content).

**Predictions (kill conditions in ledger T1/T2):** translation ⇒ near-zero fine-scale flux; identity ⇒ flux ≈ H(input detail) at fine scales (volume); recolor-by-rank ⇒ mid-scale bulge; measured spectra must match the analytic floors on constructed tasks within estimator bias, and excess flux must correlate with LoO generalization gap across ARC families.

## 3. What we are NOT claiming (reviewer-proofing)

- No AdS/CFT duality for neural networks: no large-N factorization, no strong/weak coupling, no diffeomorphism story. The Mehta–Schwab episode is the cautionary tale — and note carefully: their variational-RG↔RBM mapping was *contested, then defended* (the Lin–Tegmark counterexample violates the Kadanoff trace condition Tr_h e^{T}=1 on which exactness rests — Schwab–Mehta rejoinder). Lesson adopted: **exactness conditions stated explicitly, or the claim is labeled heuristic.**
- Engineered correspondences (Hashimoto et al. AdS/DL: holography is an *input* ansatz, network as inverse-problem solver; Gan–Shu: RT-for-DNNs asserted by analogy, min-cut↔entropy never established for trained networks) are cited as motivation, never as evidence. The HNQTWY theorem holds for *random* TNs at large bond dimension — conditions never verified for trained DNNs *anywhere in the literature* — which is precisely the measurement gap we fill.
- NN-QFT correspondences are exact only at infinite width (free theory), perturbative at finite width — used for context only.
- The scaling-hypothesis connection (T3) is stated as the **geometric reading** — exponents set by intrinsic/task-manifold dimension (Sharma–Kaplan lineage; 2025–26 RG treatments of scaling laws) — a hypothesis about our own d-sweep and adaptation-rank data, not a duality.

## 4. Novelty adjudication (from the 2024–26 sweep)

- **CompressARC** (Liao & Gu, arXiv:2512.06104): 76k params, *zero pretraining*, 20% ARC-AGI-1 eval by per-task MDL minimization at inference. The closest philosophical neighbor — global description-length, **no cut structure, no flux, no communication complexity**. Also now a mandatory empirical baseline for our parameter-golf claims (76k/20% vs our ~100k target). Engage, cite, compare.
- **ARC-AGI-2 report** (arXiv:2505.11831) measures task information content by compression; no cut-based bounds. 
- **Generalization Ridge** (arXiv:2507.05387): layer-wise predictive information vs memorization in NLG transformers — the nearest "measured flux ↔ generalization" work; depth cuts only, observational probes (not enforced channels), not ARC. Differentiate on: spatial+scale cut families, exact-by-construction channels, reasoning corpus, analytic floors.
- **Negative space confirmed:** no bit-threads/max-flow application to ML information flow found; no communication-complexity treatment of ARC or grid reasoning found. The chain's last links appear genuinely unclaimed. *(Subject to the pending mechanical re-verification pass.)*
