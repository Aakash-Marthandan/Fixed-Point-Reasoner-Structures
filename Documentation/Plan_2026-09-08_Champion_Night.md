- **INTEGRITY** (a breach on a PRESENT arm WITHHOLDS the verdict; missing rows are NO-DATA, not a breach): full_vsel_t16 n == 422,786; full_final_t16 n ∈ {422,786 (copied when vsel == final), 50,000}; full_vsel_t16_alt n == 50,000; full_vsel_t64 n == 100,000; scan n == 5,000, k 32, t64, subsample seed 20260822, unique idx, identical idx sets across every present scan; sub20k_t128 n == 20,000; sub5k_t256 n == 5,000; sub20k_t16_commit n == 20,000; every vsel-labeled eval of an arm reports ONE checkpoint path, and that path's step equals the step in `val_best.txt`.
- **CLEAN split:** STABILITY := not STOPPED AND census(vsel, t64) ≤ .02; MEMORIZATION (labeled, never disqualifying) := end segment-CE < .02 OR (cold16 vsel − cold16 final) > .05.
- **NOISE FLOOR:** FLOOR_C := max(max − min of cold16 over the STABLE members of {C0, C1, C2}, .005); fewer than 2 stable members → .015, labeled FLOOR-DEFAULT. A contrast is READ only beyond 2 × FLOOR_C; else FLAT. Beside it, descriptive: FLOOR_SCREEN := the spread of the vb screens' exact_acc over the same members.
- **R-CH-0 SEEDS:** SPREAD-C := max − min of cold16 over the stable seed arms: ≤ .015 TIGHT; ≤ .03 LOOSE; else WIDE.
- **R-CH-1 ORBIT** (C3 vs M := the stable seed arms' mean cold16): d > 2 FLOOR_C → ORBIT-LIFTS; d < −2 FLOOR_C → ORBIT-HURTS; else ORBIT-FLAT; +DELAYS iff C3's memorization onset (the first logged step with ce_in < .05; "never" if none) is ≥ 10,000 steps later than the stable seed arms' mean onset, or "never" while theirs exist; else +SAME-ONSET.
- **R-CH-2 OPERATOR** (C4 vs M): ATTENTION-HELPS (d > 2 FLOOR_C) / ATTENTION-HURTS (d < −2 FLOOR_C) / MEAN-SUFFICES.
- **R-CH-3 WIDTH-DOWN** (C5): cold64 ≥ .920 → PARITY-AT-1.3x; ≥ .874 → TRM-CLASS-AT-1.3x; else BELOW-AT-1.3x.
- **R-CH-4 COMMIT** (C6): w := commit_wrong_among_committed_unsolved_last (sub20k_t16_commit); d := cold16(C6) − M: w < .30 AND d > 2 FLOOR_C → CALIBRATED-AND-LIFTS; w < .30 AND |d| ≤ 2 FLOOR_C → CALIBRATED-INERT; w < .30 AND d < −2 FLOOR_C → CALIBRATED-AT-COST; w ≥ .30 → UNCALIBRATED (+LIFTS / +FLAT / +COST by d). The commit AUC and the committed fraction are reported.
- **R-CH-5 SELECTOR:** every present arm's SPURIOUS (the D3/E5 definition on the 5k × k32 scan: wrong draws whose latent residual lies below the median residual of the correct draws) ≤ .01 → SELECTOR-CLEAN; else SELECTOR-DIRTY: the arms. Per arm: spurious, t1r@32, verified@32, t1r/verified.
- **R-CH-6 DEPTH:** per arm the ladder cold16 (full) / cold64 (100k) / D128 (20k) / D256 (5k), labeled sets, and each row's depth_regressions; DEPTH-MONOTONE iff every present row has regressions ≤ .0005 × n; else DEPTH-REGRESSES: the arms.
- **R-CH-7 PARITY:** cold64 ≥ .920 on every stable seed arm → PARITY-x k; else PARITY-PARTIAL passing/k. HEADLINE := the mean and (max − min) of cold64 and of D128 over the stable seed arms.
- **R-CH-EXT** (descriptive audit of the registered extension rule): per arm EXTENDED yes/no, the selected step, the final budget, whether the selected step lies inside the last 4,000 steps of the FINAL budget (a peak still rising at the end).
- **CHAMPION BY RULE:** CHAMP := the stable seed arm with the highest cold64 (memorized allowed, labeled); LABELED-BEST := the highest cold64 over every present arm. "program-record" prefix only.
- **SYNC A/B** (descriptive): the two walls and the ratio wall_per_step / wall_default from `champ_sync_ab.json`.

Reader conventions recorded in the analyzer's header (not rules): FALLBACK-FINAL → vsel := the final step; "inside the last 4,000" = strictly greater than budget − 4,000; ties in CHAMP break by arm order; a C3 without metrics reads +ONSET-NO-DATA; the depth bound is inclusive.
