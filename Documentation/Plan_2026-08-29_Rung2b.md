# Rung 2b — Design (2026-08-29, pre-registration draft for PI ratification)

**Mandate (PI):** maximum mileage from one cheap d96 round before d128 commits; ops robustness so the rung-2 time sinks cannot recur. This document is the working design; the RUNG-2B LAUNCH REGISTRATION (rules locked pre-data in `tools/analyze_sportBr2b.py`, self-tested) supersedes its science section on launch day. Goal metric named (guardrail 1): **20k vote@128 @t64 breadth → the B-M2 band (≥ .85)**, with the deep lane's trainability (H-48) and the anchor's long-horizon stability (H-45) as the mechanism questions.

---

## 1. What 2b must answer before d128 spends $250–400

1. **Where does the winner's recipe top out?** C3 (T6+FPA) was stopped at its registered 20k with the funnel still rising (+16.8 pp over its last 5k). d128 must know the recipe's right training length — and B-M2 is 4.4 pp away on an unsaturated curve.
2. **Does the anchor hold the shallow map at long horizon?** H-45 says shallow recipes collapse under extended training; FPA is built to prevent exactly that. 50k = 2.5× the budget where un-anchored T6 collapsed. Either outcome is a §3 annotation.
3. **Is the deep lane recoverable?** H-48 says the free attention channel composed through T=12 at d96 is the killer, and a minimal attention toll restores trainability. If true, the deep lane un-censors (the cold ladder 19.4 → 21.2 → 25.3 → ? gets its d96 point — C1 was pacing ahead of the d64 record-holder when it died). If false, the deep lane closes for ~$80 instead of failing at d128.
4. **What is the real d96 noise floor?** Every rung-2 funnel/cold contrast rides a cross-step labeled pair. One matched-recipe, matched-step seed pair fixes the noise scale that every d128 contrast will cite.

## 3. Decision rules (to be locked verbatim in `analyze_sportBr2b.py`, self-test pre-data)

- **FN2b (noise instrument):** = max(|screen-vb(C3) − screen-vb(D2)| v256, 2 pp), matched recipe/step — replaces the cross-step 4.88. Cold noise = |cold(C3) − cold(D2)| on full t64.
- **R2b-1 (B-M2):** PHASE4 20k scan (protocol-identical: subsample seed 20260822, k128, t64) runs iff screen-vb(D1) ≥ 88.48 − FN2b (chain gate mirrors the analyzer; C3's 88.48 pinned as env constant — gates compute, never validity). Then **B-M2 iff p4@128(D1) ≥ .85**; ≥ .8057 + FN2b → FUNNEL-GROWS; within ±FN2b → LENGTH-NEUTRAL (the cheaper 20k recipe carries); below → LONGER-TRAINING-HURTS.
- **R2b-2 (H-45-with-anchor):** D1 ANCHOR-HOLDS iff retfm ≥ .9 at every monitor (ret_sched transients explicitly do NOT count — the benign settle class); any retfm < .9 → H-45-BREAKS-ANCHOR at the named step (vb-selection reads the peak; the funnel-vs-training curve is still the deliverable).
- **R2b-3 (H-48):** D3 completes 50k finite AND final retfm ≥ .9 → **H-48-SUPPORTED** (deep lane un-censored). D3 NaN → **H-48-FALSIFIED**, deep lane CLOSED at d96 (d128 = C3-class only) regardless of D4. D3 clean + retfm < .9 → DOSE-DESTABILIZES (PI consult). D4 secondary: clean → REGISTERED-LR-RESTORED; NaN → deep lane runs 5e-4 (seed confound named). **NO RETRIES anywhere this rung — one NaN = that arm stopped, final = last clean grid, automated amputation (§5).**
- **R2b-4 (deep-lane billing, iff D3 clean):** vsel-cold(D3) ≥ max(cold(D1), cold(C3)) + max(cold-noise, 1 pp) → DEEP-COLD-PAYS → D3-recipe joins d128 as the labeled cold arm; else deep lane = optional insurance.
- **CARRIERS → d128 (mechanical):** breadth carrier = argmax screen-vb over {C3@20k (88.48 banked), D1} with the FN2b guard (tie/within-noise → the cheaper 20k recipe); carrier ×2 seeds at d128 (ws8, 3.71 M, own registration); + the D3 lane per R2b-3/4. HEADLINE conventions unchanged (cold M-bands; breadth labeled; t256 rider labeled, never the headline).

**Predictions (numeric, pre-data):** D1 screen-vb ∈ [88, 94], retfm 1.0 throughout (the anchor holds), vb ∈ [40k, 50k], p4@128 ≥ .83 (B-M2 a genuine toss-up), cold ∈ [23, 26], ignition (first screen ≥ 55) at ≥ 15k (lr-phase shift). D2: screen-vb ∈ [84, 92] (FN2b ≤ 4 pp), cold ∈ [21, 23.5]. D3: completes clean; A_total ≤ 1e5 by 10k; λ_joint max ≤ 2.5; η → [.80, .90] (closing the censored rung-2 prediction); cold vsel ∈ [24.5, 28] (the censored trajectory's extrapolation — if it lands, the deep arm reclaims the d96 cold record); screen-vb ∈ [70, 85]. D4: clean with ~60 % credence. Failure-mode predictions registered so either branch is a finding.

## 4. Instruments

- Monitors every 2k (standing); λ_joint logged as the z-calm readout (not an alarm — no reliable alarm exists for the NaN class; that is H-48's point).
- **Screens (strat-512 k256):** D1 at {10k, 15k, 20k, 25k, 40k, vb} — the ignition-replication + the 2.5×-length funnel curve; D2 at {10k, 15k, vb=20k} — the matched-pair funnel *curve* (ignition-timing noise across seeds, not just endpoint); D3/D4 at {25k, 40k, vb} (50k-arm convention).
- Cheap evals per arm (strat t6/64/256, val, ret, retfm); full t64 on all four finals (+vb where different); **no t6 fulls** (not decision-bearing — trim).
- PHASE4 20k scan: conditional per R2b-1 (D1 only; any other arm out-screening D1 and C3 = analyzer-flagged PI decision, never an automatic scan — the rung-2 pattern). t256 depth rider on the p4 arm: OPTIONAL, never blocks completion.
- Probes with the extended ε-ladder on D1/D3 (+D4 if clean) for continuity + the **graded-recovery analysis offline** ($0 — iterations-to-recovery from the already-recorded `retained_per_step`; instrument-fix option 1, analysis-side only, zero chain change). The watershed-interpolation probe (option 3) stays registered-unbuilt for rung 3.
- Free from train rows: A_total/I_total trajectories (the dose-efficacy readout), fpa_ce, rule_H.

## 5b. BUDGET-HARDENING (2026-08-29 PI directive: "don't waste the budget like rung 2" — spend capped BY CONSTRUCTION, science protected by the resume-complete bank)

Where rung-2's ≈$250–350 overrun actually went, and the structural answer to each:

| Rung-2 overrun component (≈) | Structural fix in 2b |
|---|---|
| Optimistic estimate (clean-run cost was ~$250+ at real d96 walls, registered $150–250) | 2b's estimate is built from MEASURED d96 paces; the canary re-prices it in hour 1 (see B1) |
| NaN retry cycles (~4.4 h of re-trained worker time + surgery calendar) | one-shot arms + automated amputation — the class is gone |
| PHASE4 claim-race (16-way ran 4-way → ~4× tail wall) + silent shard reruns | claim cap/TTL/stamps + spawn echoes + count-invariants (§5) |
| Multi-day exposure (campaign crossed 2.5 days of churn windows; 6 node losses, most in US-day hours) | night-window launch + the CHURN CIRCUIT-BREAKER (B3) |
| Idle/zombie waste (false-verified teardown ~$14; orphan gsutil; C4_vb repair node ~$7) | positive-describe teardown; close-out sweeps; the zero-byte guard port (B4) |

**B1 — Projection tripwire at hour 1 (stronger than the spend tripwire).** The first arm's canary-measured pace re-prices the whole campaign immediately. If the PROJECTED total exceeds the registered top ($270) by >20 %, the supervisor holds after PHASE1 banking and the heartbeat presents pause/continue to the PI — the decision moves from "1.5× spend already burned" (rung-2's O8) to "1.2× projected, ~$40 burned."

**B2 — Max-spend-by-construction: the deadline IS the budget cap.** `runs/tpu_deadline.txt` set at launch to window-start + 22 h (16-shape; per-window +12 h on the 8-shape) and NOT extended without an explicit PI go — the supervisor stops creating past it and the launchd watchdog hard-deletes past it. Worst-case node spend is therefore ≈ $300 (16) / ≈ $150 per window (8) no matter what goes wrong — rung 2 overran because nothing could stop it; 2b physically cannot exceed ~1.4× its registered top silently. Science protection: the chain is resume-complete with ≤10-min-loss banking, and every arm's stopping semantics are registered — a cap-stop yields a labeled partial campaign that RESUMES on PI approval, never a loss.

**B3 — Churn circuit-breaker (2b-specific posture, PI-overridable per event).** ≥2 node losses within 2 h during US-day hours → the supervisor STOPS hunting until the next night window and notifies the PI (who can reply "keep hunting" — the rung-2 default inverted: pause is the default, riding is the exception). Trades calendar (bounded by the deadline cap) for dollars; the spot-churn-temperament policy stands for night-window churn, where losses are rare and cheap. Hunt posture: 16-first for 90 min from window start, then take the 8 (never hammer a dry ladder through the day).

**B4 — Completion guard hardened against the zero-byte class (the C4_vb lesson, ~$7 + a repair session).** The node-side completion guard now RE-DERIVES skip-legitimacy before emitting the sentinel: a zero-byte screen/eval object passes ONLY if the local `val_best.txt`/STOPPED state says that skip is legal (mstep == vb step, or mstep > a stopped arm's final step); any other zero-byte or missing object BLOCKS the sentinel → the supervisor keeps the warm node and the defect is fixed for minutes of node time instead of a cold re-provision. The full Mac-side audit (`--tag sportBr2b`) still gates §6 as usual.

**B5 — Frozen chain.** No mid-campaign feature work (rung-2 shipped fixes during the ride out of necessity; 2b's chain is FROZEN at launch after the harness pass). Live interventions are limited to the registered emergency verbs: kill-orphan-process, extend-deadline-with-PI-go, pause. A live defect → pause-and-fix-offline per B3 semantics, never live-patching.

**B6 — Spend-vs-plan curve in every heartbeat.** The registered estimate becomes a piecewise expected-spend curve (PHASE1 banked by ~T+12 h ≈ $165; evals/screens by ~T+16 h ≈ $220; conditional PHASE4 by ~T+19 h); each heartbeat reports measured spend AND projected total against it, so deviations localize to a phase within the hour they appear instead of surfacing in the close-out accounting.

**What is deliberately NOT done to save money:** no science descope (all four arms + registered instruments stand); no mid-campaign node downsizing to harvest the last ~$20 of idle tail (a stop/resume cycle costs 25–40 min of bring-up+compile risk — the rung-2 lesson that swaps below ~$50 of savings are net-negative); no quality reduction anywhere (statistics, screens, audit unchanged). Hardening buys dollars and calendar; it never buys statistics.

**Pre-mortem highlights (full pass at build):** early D3/D4 NaN → workers roll into eval claims, campaign completes early and cheap; D1 vb lands ≤ 20k → fixed-step screens still run, vb screen reads the peak, R2b-1 uses it correctly; shape change mid-PHASE4 → pinned partition (proven); D2 flag inheritance → explicit arm_flags pin + admission at ckpt level; cross-rung contamination → own tag/dirs/GCS prefix + `audit --tag sportBr2b` extended with the same stopped-arm semantics.
