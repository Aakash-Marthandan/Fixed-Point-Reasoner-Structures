# Registration: is the fast state slaved to the slow one? (2026-09-19 ~16:10Z, before the data)

**Why.** The PI wants RG kept as the paper's central reference mechanism, tested rather than asserted (`paper/documentation/PAPER_SPINE_PROPOSAL_2026-09-19.md`, "a useful bounded additional probe"; `PAPER_SPINE_MERGED_2026-09-19.md`, prediction P4). Of the reference's predictions, one is about the ARCHITECTURE: the nested loop (six updates of the fast state z_L at fixed slow state z_H, then one update of z_H, three times per iteration) is read, in the draft's Appendix C, as a separation of timescales in which "the fast variable is adiabatically eliminated". The draft says the relaxation-time instrument "is not reported here". This probe reports it. Goal: a MEASUREMENT for paper 1. $0, the Mac's CPU (at low priority beside the corpus run), banked checkpoints, nothing trained.

**Known before this registration.** That unsolved trajectories amplify a change of compiled kernel while solved ones do not (the corpus note's Amendment 1); the released models' finite-difference Jacobian radius at 64-iteration endpoints (EqR 0.76 solved / 5.6 unsolved; TRM 1.01 / 4.2; FieldCheckpoints report §8), which is a statement about the WHOLE iteration map at the end of a run, not about the fast loop at fixed slow state. NOT known: anything about the decay of a fast-state difference inside an iteration, on any model; what resetting either state at iteration boundaries does.

**What.** `tools/lens_fast_slow.py` (the model's own segment replicated one iteration at a time, as the corpus lens's Part C, with the same gate: equal to the evaluator on more than 99.9 % of the empty cells at iteration 1 and on the puzzles the evaluator has solved through iteration 4; selftest on a toy map with a known contraction). Models: ours at width 192 (C5, 46k), ours at width 384 (C0, 16k), TRM reproduced (X0), EqR's released weights. Puzzles: the validity lens's 256 rating-stratified test puzzles; the fixed start; no noise; 16 iterations.
- **Part 1, inside one iteration.** Along the unperturbed trajectory, at the START of iterations t = 1, 2, 4, 8: copy the state, perturb ONLY the fast state, z_L' = z_L + ε · rms(z_L) · ξ with ξ standard normal and ε = 0.1, and (for t > 1) a second variant that replaces z_L by its initial buffer. Run both copies through the iteration. Record, per puzzle: δ_l = ‖z_L' − z_L‖ / ‖z_L‖ after l = 0 … 6 inner updates of the FIRST outer cycle (the slow state is identical in both copies there, so this is the fast map's contraction at fixed slow state); Δ_1 and Δ_3 = ‖z_H' − z_H‖ / ‖z_H‖ after the first outer cycle and at the iteration's end; the share of empty cells whose output digit differs at the iteration's end. Groups, by the unperturbed trajectory: already solved at the start of iteration t; not yet solved and solved by 16; never solved by 16.
- **Part 2, across iterations.** Sixteen iterations with z_L reset to its initial buffer at the start of EVERY iteration (only the slow state is carried), and, separately, with z_H reset (only the fast state is carried); exact at 16 against the unperturbed run, paired. These are inference-time interventions outside the training distribution and are labeled so.

DESCRIPTIVE; adjudicates no registered row of any campaign. ε, the branch iterations and the groups are fixed here.

**Predictions (credences; "contraction" = the median over puzzles of δ_6 / δ_0 at ε = 0.1).**
- F1 on already-solved puzzles the fast loop contracts (median δ_6 / δ_0 < 1) on all four models: 0.80; strongly (≤ 0.1): 0.45.
- F2 at t = 4 and t = 8 the contraction is weaker (a larger δ_6 / δ_0) on not-yet-solved than on already-solved puzzles, on all four models: 0.75.
- F3 on not-yet-solved puzzles the fast loop EXPANDS (median δ_6 / δ_0 ≥ 1) on at least one model: 0.45.
- F4 carrying only the slow state (z_L reset every iteration) changes accuracy at 16 by less than 2 points on all four models: 0.35. Carrying only the fast state (z_H reset) leaves less than half of the unperturbed accuracy on all four: 0.80.
- F5 on already-solved puzzles a fast-state perturbation barely reaches the slow state: median Δ_3 / δ_0 < 0.1 on all four: 0.55.

**What the paper says under each outcome (written before the data).**
- **F1 strong AND F4's first clause hold:** "within an iteration the fast state forgets its initialization (a difference shrinks by a factor x over the six inner updates), and across iterations only the slow state needs to be carried: the reduction of the dynamics to the slow variable is a measured property of these models." This is the one row of the RG correspondence the record would then support as measured, and it is about timescales, not about fixed cells.
- **F1 holds only on solved puzzles, F2 / F3 hold:** "the fast state is slaved to the slow one once a puzzle is solved and not before: the reduction describes the endpoint, not the search that reaches it."
- **F1 fails:** "the nested loop is not a separation of timescales in these models"; the adiabatic paragraph leaves Appendix C, and the paper says so as a result.
- **F4 (either clause), whichever way:** reported as which state carries the computation across iterations; no mechanism word is attached.
- Under every outcome: one checkpoint per model, 256 puzzles, a perturbation of one size; a contraction of differences is not a proof of a fixed point, and none will be claimed.

## Outcome (2026-09-19 ~20:30Z; `runs/analysis/fast_slow_20260919/{report.txt, report.json}`; all four models present; every probe equal to the evaluator on ≥ 99.99 % of empty cells at iteration 1 and 100 % on solved puzzles through iteration 4; selftest 7/7, three mutants killed)

**Scoreboard: 2 of 7 registered lines.** F1 (contracts on already-solved puzzles, all four) HELD; F1-strong (≤ 0.1) FAILED (ours 0.08–0.26 at width 192, 0.04–0.11 at width 384; TRM reproduced 0.02–0.04; EqR 0.05). F2 FAILED on one cell of eight: ours at width 192, iteration 4, 0.2556 not-yet-solved against 0.2564 already-solved, a tie; the other seven hold, with the released-class models far apart (TRM reproduced 0.27 against 0.02; EqR 0.22 against 0.05). F3 HELD, but only at iteration 1 from the untrained fixed start (a fast-state perturbation grows 2.9–4.8× on all four); from iteration 2 on the fast loop contracts on every group of every model. F4 FAILED both ways: carrying only the slow state costs 2.3–3.5 points (ours −3.5 and −2.3, TRM reproduced −2.7, EqR −3.5; I predicted under 2); carrying only the fast state leaves 8.6 % (EqR), 20.3 % (TRM reproduced), 34.8 % (ours, width 384) and 61.7 % (ours, width 192), not under half on ours. F5 FAILED on one cell of twelve: ours at width 192, iteration 4, 0.1001 against a threshold of 0.1; the others read 0.004–0.06. The letters are reported as registered; the two one-cell misses are within 0.001 of their thresholds and are named as such, not re-scored.

**What it shows, descriptively.** After the first iteration the fast loop forgets a perturbation of its own state on every group of puzzles (a factor 0.02–0.33 over the six inner updates). What separates solved from unsolved puzzles is what reaches the SLOW state and the output: on solved puzzles, 0.4–10 % of the perturbation reaches the slow state by the iteration's end and no output cell changes on any model (0 of 47–225 puzzles per branch); on puzzles not yet solved, 23–53 % reaches it (82–108 % on puzzles never solved on our models) and 1.4–5.8 % of output cells change. So the solved state is locally insensitive to the fast variable and the unsolved state is not. Resetting the fast state every iteration costs little (2–4 points) on all four; the slow state carries most of what persists across iterations, fully so on EqR and TRM reproduced and less so on ours.

**For the paper (by the rule written before the data).** F1 held only weakly and F2 held on 7 of 8 cells, so neither registered branch applies cleanly. The supportable sentence is the descriptive one above: the reduction to the slow variable describes the solved endpoint, not the search that reaches it. No fixed-point or RG claim follows (a contraction of differences under one perturbation size, one checkpoint per model, 256 stratified puzzles). The draft's "the slow state carries the commitment while the fast one propagates" is not supported as a division of labour: the fast state alone carries a third to three fifths of our models' accuracy.
