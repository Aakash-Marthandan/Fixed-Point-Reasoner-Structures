## 2. The findings

### 2.1 Breadth scales — and the funnel changes owners a second time, back to the shallow map
The program's first BREADTH-SCALES verdict: **80.57 % vote@128 on the full-test-grade 20k subsample** (CI ±0.55), +11.95 pp over the d16 bar that d64 could not clear (66.16). Per-octile vote@128 dominates **every** reference on **every** octile — hardest octile (rating ≥48): **62.9 %** vs 48.5 (d16) / 41.6 (d64). The curve is unsaturated (+10.2 pp on the last doubling; strat v256 = 88.48). The ownership history is now: d16 → T6-plain owns breadth (T12 narrow); d64 → T12-FPA owns it (T6 broken by H-45); d96 → **T6-FPA owns it** (FPA fixed what broke T6 at width; width fixed what limited T6 at d16). Ownership tracks *whichever recipe is stable at that scale*, not a monotone hand-off to depth — and the stable-cheap recipe (T6@20k trains ~5× cheaper than T12@50k) now holds the crown. At matched 20k steps and matched screens, T6-FPA out-funnels T12-FPA at d96 (88.48 vs 71.29).

### 2.2 The free deep carrier dies by state explosion — and the flux ledger predicts who survives
Both carrier seeds NaN'd twice (registered lr and half-lr; one-retry rule stopped both). The forensics (GCS live-metrics tails, labeled): **single-window cliffs with zero y-side precursor** — C1's train loss 0.43–0.51 and val at its *best-ever* (0.172) at step 22,050, then loss 2610 and I/A_total at ~1e14 one 50-step window later; retfm 1.00 and λ_y 0.20 (deeply contractive) at the last monitor. λ_joint (the z-scratchpad modes) was the only moving instrument on C1 (1.75 → 4.09 → 4.55 across 18k→22k) — but C1s1 died with λ_joint ≈ 0.96, so no monitored precursor is reliable; grad-clip 1.0 was active throughout (clip refuted as the fix). **The survival pattern maps onto the ledger**: the free T12 arms carried ~1–2.3 M stream + ~10–11 M attention nats and died 4/4 attempts; the priced arms (attention channel fully shut, A ≈ 0.3–1.3 nats) sailed through 50k at both β; free T6 (C3) carried *more* attention flux (A ≈ 19.6 M) yet survived — the composed depth (12 vs 6 recursion steps), not the flux alone, is the second factor. Reading: **unpriced attention flux composed through deep recursion at width = the instability**; the July C14 warning ("the unpriced channel is a loophole magnet; keep β_nl > 0") expresses at d96 as a *trainability* failure, and H-44's stability dividend upgrades from "keeps the equilibrium contractive" to "keeps training finite." Registered as **[H-48]** with the rung-2b β_nl-dose cell as its test (§5). Honest confound: all four NaNs were on FPA arms (free-T12-*plain* at d96 is unmeasured); the dose cell changes one variable from C1, so either outcome attributes cleanly.

### 2.3 Priced breadth: the conjecture dies, the budget direction survives sub-threshold
P-A and P-B both failed → **PRICED-BREADTH-DEAD** by the registered adjudication; the PI conjecture's strong form does not fire at these scales. What the screens actually show: C2 (std-β) is **condensed-stationary at every checkpoint** (30.5/33.4/33.0, ratios ~1.25) — the d64 mid-training wide window (B3@25k = 71.3) did **not** replicate at d96 (C2@25k = 30.5), killing the "width delays condensation" reading at n=2 (the d64 window was checkpoint luck or d64-specific). C4 (β/3) **grows monotonically and de-flattens** (27.2 → 33.6 → 43.0; ratio 1.07 → 1.38) — the budget axis does move diversity in the right direction, just nowhere near the 55 bar. The throat is nearly β-inelastic across the tested decade-third (train-convention 871 → 1319 nats, +51 % for 3× lower β) — C4 buys its diversity at modestly higher rate, so H-47's "competition for nats" survives only in its weak, slow, budget-mediated form. Consequence: **priced funnels are not a d128 lane**; the toll's roles remain stability + compression (which at d96 became load-bearing for survival itself, §2.2).

### 2.4 The funnel is a dynamical object: ignition, oscillation, and the Fisher migration
The ≥3-checkpoint screens (this rung's new instrument) show the funnel *forming*: C3 ignites between 10k and 15k (29.7 → 71.7 → 88.48, still rising at its registered end) — and the ignition coincides with (i) the λ_joint settle after a turbulent transient (λ_J > 1 with ret_sched dips to .08–.58 through 10k, settled by 12k — the d16 A2 "settle" phenomenology, now on T6-FPA at width) and (ii) the **Fisher eq→coding migration onset** (eq-share 75 % → 53 % over exactly 10k→15k). On the *unstable* free-deep arm the funnel **oscillates**: C1 = 50.4 (10k) → 27.2 (15k, flat-k — a condensation) → 71.3 (20k, reopened), with λ_joint never settling (1.7–4.6) — funnel instability, z-mode turbulence, and eventual state death travel together. C1s1 ignited by 10k (21.5 → 76.2). H-46-funnel at d96: the checkpoint-sensitive object confirmed, with sign structure — monotone growth on stable arms (C3 +58.8, C4 +15.8), stationarity on priced-std (2.9), oscillation on the doomed free-deep arm. Single-checkpoint funnel numbers on unstable recipes are near-meaningless; the multi-ckpt screen is the instrument of record.

- *Is BREADTH-SCALES driven by easy puzzles?* No — the per-octile table shows the d96 gain is largest on the hard tail (hardest octile +14.4 pp over d16, +21.3 over d64).
- *Could C3's funnel keep growing past 20k, or collapse (H-45)?* Unknown — its registered length ended at 20k with the funnel still rising (+16.8 pp over the last 5k). Both continuations are live; this is rung-2b's cheapest, highest-value cell (§5).
- *Sample sizes*: p4 n=20000 (CI ±0.55 pp at .8); screens n=512 (CI ≈ ±2 pp at .85 — the C3-vs-carrier screen gaps are 6–8× CI); cold contrasts are n=1 vs n=1 at the known 1.5–7 pp noise scale and none is claim-bearing alone; the seed pair is cross-step (labeled). - *The 80.57 % provenance*: standard PHASE4 protocol on the audited final ckpt of the winner, partition-pinned, n-gated, subsample-seed-identical to both references — the cleanest breadth provenance of the program.
