**Why aug 1000 did not help (mechanism, labeled):** the memorization step is nearly the same as the pilot's (d96, aug 100: CE → 0 by 40k; d128, aug 1000: by 32–40k) although the corpus is 10× more diverse and the epoch count 10× lower (5.1 vs 32 epochs at 40k). At 64 × 40k = 2.56M samples the map sees each of the 1,001,000 augmented pairs ~2.6 times — and still reaches CE 0. The position-group augmentation multiplies copies of the SAME 1,000 solutions under a group the map can learn (the group is a fixed permutation structure of rows/bands/columns/stacks; the mixer's per-slot embeddings learn it), so the corpus carries ~1k puzzles of information whatever the augmentation count: memorization = "learn the 1,000 solutions as a lookup on the givens pattern, equivariantly". The field's aug-1000 convention is therefore not the anti-memorization lever by itself; **weight decay is**: R0 (wd 1.0, lr 1e-4, batch 384, 50k steps = 19.2 epochs of the same corpus) ends at CE .423 (min .342), its monitor val is still RISING (+6.25pp over the last 10k), and its stream flux is 5× smaller (I_total 30k nats vs 160–190k on the memorizing twins — the S2 reading "excess free flux is memorization" in its regularization-side form) with a codebook that never fully committed (rule_H .27 vs 0.000 on every wd-1e-4 arm). This is the grokking literature's claim (weight decay converts memorization into generalization on small algorithmic datasets) measured on our cell with the basin/flux instruments. **Val-selection remains the instrument of record:** it caught both peaks (B0 A:20k, B1 A:20k) and those grids are the program's best cold numbers (§4).

**X0n (the field cell without ACT) memorizes too** (CE .0075 at 50k, `train_exact` .999, val peak .72 @24k → .625, full cold 72.08 vsel → 64.18 final) at 38 segment-passes per sample with wd 1.0 — while X0 (with ACT) does not (segment CE .60 at 50k, `train_exact` .20 at halting, val still rising to .875 @48k). **Consequence (H-49 sharpened, the fired kill's registered consequence):** the remedy set for our cell at d128 must include the regularization axis — wd (1.0 vs 1e-4) is the measured lever; lr 1e-4 vs 1e-3 and batch are confounded with it inside R0 and need the one-variable decomposition the plan §12.3 named ("if R0 wins, the next night decomposes the regime").
