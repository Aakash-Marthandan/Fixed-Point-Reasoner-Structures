**Status: REGISTERED, NOT LAUNCHED.** §3 (rules) and §4 (expectations and credences) lock at this file's registration commit; the analyzer `tools/analyze_x5long.py` is frozen from the same commit. The launch needs the PI's confirmation of this page and a go.

## 1. Page one: the goal, the expected accuracy, what each outcome means

**The goal.** Paper 1 credits the symmetric DEC's accuracy to its cell, not to its size or recipe. The row that tests this is TRM's cell at the DEC's parameter count under the DEC's full recipe (X5). Its first reading (45.0 % at 16 iterations, 47.5 % at 64) was taken at the edge of a 50k-step budget it had not finished using, at one nineteenth of the DEC's training compute (`Report_2026-09-17_Sudoku_Pending_Verdict.md`, §4). This run gives the same model the DEC's training compute and reads it again. It is a control for the paper's attribution claim, not an attempt at a best TRM number.

**What is run.** X5's banked 50k state (weights, EMA weights, optimizer state, RNG) resumed under its unchanged recipe to **960,000 steps** — 50,000 × 272.7 / 14.2, the measured training paces of X5 and of the width-192 DEC on the same 8-chip pod type. That is 19 times X5's first budget. Then the same evaluation battery as every champion arm, on the checkpoint the registered selector picks.

**Expected accuracy (mine, stated before the data).**

| | X5 at 50k (measured) | **X5 at 960k (expected)** | the DEC triple (measured) | TRM's cell at 5 M on this loop (measured) | TRM as published |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 iterations, all 422,786 | 45.04 | **median 66, 80 % interval 52–84** | 95.41 ± 0.54 | 85.65–87.79 | 87.4 (its protocol) |
| 64 iterations, 100k | 47.47 | **median 69, 80 % interval 54–87** | 99.01 ± 0.18 | — | — |

I expect a large gain over the 50k row and a result still well below the DEC. I give 5 % to parity with the triple at 16 iterations.

**What each outcome will mean for the paper (committed now).**

| Outcome | The paper says |
|---|---|
| BELOW and PLATEAUED | At matched parameters, recipe and training compute, TRM's cell plateaus at X / Y. The attribution to the cell stands, with its number. |
| BELOW and STILL-RISING | At matched parameters and training compute TRM's cell reaches X / Y and is still improving (g points per 300k steps). The claim is made at matched compute only; no asymptotic claim. |
| PARITY or ABOVE | The accuracy attribution to the cell is withdrawn: at equal parameters and equal training compute the two cells are then equal in accuracy. What remains for the DEC is the restart / selector rows and the mechanism findings. Seeds 1 and 2 of X5 are owed before any wording is final. |
| A memorization onset inside the budget | The row reports the selected peak and the onset step; it joins the memorization-clock discussion. |

**Cost.** ≈ 1–1.5 h of training, ≈ 1–1.5 h of battery, the k128 row, boot and preflight: **≈ 3–4 h of one spot v6e-8, ≈ $25–35; ≈ $60 with preemptions.**

**For the PI to confirm:** (a) the goal as stated; (b) the budget is the compute-matched 960k, run in full rather than stopped at the first plateau — it costs at most ≈ $10 more, keeps the whole curve, and makes the plateau a registered *reading* (R-XL-1) instead of a stopping decision; (c) one seed, with seeds 1–2 owed only if the result lands within ten points of the triple.

## 5. Known limits, stated before the data

1. **One seed.** The BELOW letter is robust to seed noise if the gap stays in the tens of points. Inside ten points of the triple it is not, and seeds 1–2 are owed.
2. **Compute is matched by measured training pace on the same pod type** — a wall-clock proxy for FLOPs that includes the input pipeline and the halting behaviour. It matches *training* compute only. At inference the DEC also costs about nineteen times more per iteration; this run does not address that, and the paper should say so where it states the row.
3. **The plateau reading is bounded by the budget.** A 300k-step window would miss a later stair. The claim this run supports ends at "matched training compute".
4. **The monitor is 512 puzzles** (a standard error of about 2 points near 50 %). R-XL-1 reads a running maximum over many rows, not a single row; the selected grid's true accuracy is read on the full test set, not on the monitor.
5. **Selection resolution** is 10k steps past 50k against 2k before it. The instrument is unchanged.
6. **Preemptions** add labeled resumes; each restarts the SOT carry and re-runs at most 5,000 steps.
