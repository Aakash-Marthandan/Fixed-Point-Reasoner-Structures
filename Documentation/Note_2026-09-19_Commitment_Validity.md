# Registration: is "commitment" a property of the models or of the normalization? (2026-09-19, before the data)

> **Adversarial audit addendum (2026-09-19):** The StableMax correction survives. The subsequent narrative must use audit F03–F07: restricted first-hit alignment, endpoint versus any-later revision, full-vocabulary/precision limitations, and calibration versus discrimination. This note remains a historical registration and outcome record. See [the audit](Report_2026-09-19_Adversarial_Writing_Audit.md).

**Why.** The outside audit of the paper (GPT-6 Astra, 2026-09-19; `runs/external_audit_2026-09-19/astra_audit.md` §3(d)) raised that the models are trained under **stablemax** cross-entropy while every commitment number in the paper is a **softmax** confidence above 0.9. Checked at the source before this registration: `tools/suite_ckpt.py` l.40–44 and `tools/lens_repair_radius.py` l.204–208 take `softmax(logits)`, renormalize over the nine digits and threshold the maximum at 0.9; the run configs (`runs/*/config.json`) read `loss_kind = stablemax` on EVERY loop model we trained (37 `dec`, 11 `trm`) and `loss_kind = softmax` on EVERY control (`rg`, 19 runs); the released checkpoints are TRM-family code (stablemax per their repositories; not re-verified here). No local run breaks the pairing: there is no loop model trained under softmax and no control trained under stablemax. So the paper's class contrast ("every model built on the recursion commits 85–99 % of the empty cells at iteration 1; the controls 3–13 %") is confounded with the training loss, and a softmax read of stablemax-trained logits can saturate where the model's own probability does not (logits (5, 0, …, 0): softmax 0.949, stablemax 0.429). Goal: a MEASUREMENT for paper 1 that decides how §1, the first claim unit, Table 3 row 1, Fig. 2 and Definition 4–5 may be worded. $0, the Mac's CPU, banked checkpoints, nothing trained.

**Known before this registration.** The corpus's softmax commitment shares (`corpus_lens_20260909.csv`), the why-decimation lens's F1/F2 rows (softmax), the first-guess wrong share and the cells-correct curves (argmax quantities; normalization-free already). NOT known: any stablemax-normalized confidence, any logit margin or logit scale, on any model.

**What.** `tools/lens_commit_validity.py` (its own loop, mirroring `suite_ckpt.run_dyn` so the `rg` controls load; imports the evaluator's `_step`, `coupled_ab`, `layout_gather`; selftest with hand-built records, the formatter asserted, and the numpy stablemax asserted equal to `qhrrn2.objective.log_stablemax`). Evaluation as the paper's: the fixed start, no halting, no noise, 16 iterations, the weights each corpus row used (EMA on the loop models, raw on the controls, as `suite_ckpt_dyn_*.jsonl` records). Puzzles: the 256 rating-stratified test puzzles (`stratified_subsample`, seed 20260821, as the corpus and the repair-radius lens). The digit logits of every iteration are SAVED (float16), so any later transform is an offline read of the same trajectories, not a new run.
- Loop models (stablemax-trained): C5 (symmetric, width 192, 46k), C0 (symmetric, width 384, 16k), X0 (TRM's network reproduced, 5M, 50k), EQR (EqR's released weights, ported), SA256 (SE-RRM's mixers in our recipe, 28k).
- Controls (softmax-trained, no nested loop): B0 (`sportC1/B0@A20k`, the best control, 45 % at 64), W0 (`sportC2/W0@vsel`), R1 (`sportC2/R1@vsel`, the one control trained with deep supervision).
- Reads, over the EMPTY cells, per puzzle then averaged: (a) the committed share at iterations 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 under **softmax** and under **stablemax** (both over the nine digit logits) at thresholds 0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 0.99; (b) wrong among the committed at iteration 1 (denominator = the committed cells) under each; (c) normalization-free: the first-guess wrong share, the share of empty cells whose iteration-1 digit is the final digit, the share whose digit never changes after iteration 1, the median top logit and the median top-minus-second margin; (d) the mean top confidence at iteration 16 on solved against unsolved puzzles under each (the confidence gap), with the number of unsolved puzzles printed; (e) the cell-level AUC of "this cell is wrong" at iteration 1 from each confidence. OWN normalization = stablemax for the loop models, softmax for the controls.

DESCRIPTIVE; nothing here adjudicates a registered row. The three transforms (softmax, stablemax, raw margin) are fixed here; no other transform will be reported as primary.

## Outcome (2026-09-19 ~13:30Z; `runs/analysis/commit_validity_20260919/{report.txt, report.json, posthoc.txt, posthoc.json, run.log}`; the tool's selftest 13/13 with two mutants killed before the run; every number below is printed by the tool)

**The run.** Eight models, 256 puzzles, 16 iterations, ≈ 21 min on the Mac's CPU, $0. The digit logits of every iteration are saved per model (`<key>.npz`, float16), so every transform below is a read of the same trajectories. The tool asserts, per step, that its digit argmax equals the evaluator's on the empty cells (> 99.5 %).

**Scoreboard: 5 of 8.** P1 FAILED and P2 FAILED (own-normalization commitment at 0.9, iteration 1: C5 24.0, C0 38.2, X0 31.4, EQR 22.0, SA256 39.9 %; the same cells under softmax: 92.7–96.7 %). P3 HELD (controls ≤ 2.4 % under both transforms). P4 HELD (22.0 % against 2.4 %). P5 HELD (C5: wrong among the committed 36.6 → 8.1 %). P6 HELD on all five (mean top probability at 16, unsolved against solved: 46–51 % against 99–100 % under stablemax; 96–97 % against 100 % under softmax). P7 FAILED (the wrong-cell AUC at iteration 1 is higher under stablemax by 0.04–0.05 on three models). P8 HELD (median margins 19.9–37.7 logits against 0.4). My two misses share one cause: I priced the own-normalized share at "probably above half" because I expected the logit scale (it is large: margins of 20–38) to saturate stablemax too; stablemax's polynomial tails do not saturate at that scale.

**The registered outcome is ARTIFACT**, and the consequences written above apply: the 85–99 % figure and "high-confidence commitment at the first iteration" leave the claim-level story; the mechanism rests on the argmax quantities; the class contrast carries its confound sentence; "knows that it has failed, not where" is kept because P6 held (and is stronger under the training normalization: it needs no trained head).

**What the registered rows show beyond the letters.** Under its training normalization a loop model's committed share GROWS with iteration (C5: 24 → 46 → 74 → 86 → 94 % at iterations 1, 2, 4, 8, 16; all five alike), where the softmax read is saturated from iteration 1. Wrong among the committed at iteration 1 falls from 28–37 % (softmax) to 5.4–8.1 % (own). The normalization-free rows: the first guess is wrong on 27–37 % of the empty cells of puzzles later solved; the iteration-1 digit is the final digit on 63–73 % of empty cells (controls 47–52 %), and never changes on 55–67 % (controls 25–33 %).

**POST HOC (not registered; written after the first registered rows were seen; `posthoc.txt`; exploratory).** (a) The model's own probability is well calibrated: calibration error 0.012–0.056 at iteration 1 and 0.029–0.051 on the unsolved puzzles at 16, where the softmax read gives 0.27–0.36 and 0.47–0.52. The paper's "0.501 against the correctness head's 0.013" compared a head with the wrong transform. (b) On the puzzles a model fails, it is sure of 2.5–6.7 % of the empty cells at 16 (softmax: 86–92 %), and of those 0–5.6 % are wrong (softmax: 48–54 %); the wrong-cell AUC there is 0.63–0.69 under either read. (c) What a model is sure of is right and is kept: at iteration 1 the sure cells are 2.0–4.5 % wrong and 1.7–3.5 % of them are later revised; from iteration 2 both are ≤ 0.6 %, and from iteration 4 both are 0.0 % on all five models. The cells it is not sure of are 44–58 % wrong, and that is where the repair happens. The best control shows a slower, less precise version (sure of 2 → 42 % by iteration 8, 13 % of them wrong there).

**Critique.** One checkpoint per model, 256 puzzles, 16 iterations, the fixed start; five loop models of three kinds (ours at two widths, an attention variant, TRM reproduced, EqR's released weights) agree to within 18 points on every row, far outside any seed spread in this program, so the DIRECTION is not in doubt; the corpus-level NUMBERS are owed. The 0.9 threshold is arbitrary under either transform (at 0.5 the own-normalized share at iteration 1 is 69–80 %); the paper should report the curve, not one threshold. Stablemax is "the model's own probability" because it is the normalization of the training loss; nothing in the model consumes it at inference. The control contrast remains confounded (loss, deep supervision, halting, loop). (c) is post hoc and must be registered before it is claimed.

**Owed (registered next, before any run): the corpus under the training normalization** (the 50 checkpoints' dynamics with logits saved, the stall statistics, the joint readout on the correctness-head model, the why-lens F2 on its two models). It decides the numbers behind the paper's first two claim units, Table 3's first two rows, Fig. 2a / 2c, Definitions 4–7 and Appendix C's ε_t. The plan: `paper/documentation/WRITING_PLAN_2026-09-19.md` §0.1, §9 A1.

**Lesson (for the register).** An instrument that reads a model's confidence must use the normalization of the model's training loss, and a class contrast is only as clean as its least-matched training choice. The mismatch sat in `suite_ckpt.py` from 2026-09-07 and passed every selftest, because the selftests checked the arithmetic of the read and not what the read means for a stablemax-trained model. An outside auditor found it from the code in one pass.

## Outcome, second block (2026-09-19 evening; POST HOC, exploratory; prompted by the PI: "if the models are not mostly committing in the first step, how do they solve most of it in the first few steps? So the confidence really is a good belief reading?"). Reads of the saved logits and of the repair lens's banked records; inline scripts, numbers as printed; to be moved into the tool with selftests before anything here is quoted in the paper.

**1. The own-normalized probability is a calibrated belief.** Reliability over the empty cells, the width-192 model, all 16 iterations pooled (says → right): 27.8 → 28.3 %, 40.9 → 40.0, 58.1 → 59.7, 78.2 → 76.2, 97.0 → 97.5, 99.7 → 100.0. **2. CORRECTION of block one's reading (c) ("a sure set that grows"): the growth is across puzzles, not inside a puzzle.** On puzzles NOT yet solved at iteration t the empty cells right stay at 47–63 % for every t and the model is sure of 3–8 % of them (8–21 % at iteration 1); on puzzles already solved it is sure of 97–100 %. Aligned to the iteration f at which a puzzle first becomes fully right (f in 4..14; 46–87 puzzles per model; five models): cells right 45–54 % at f − 3, 49–53 % at f − 2, 57–63 % at f − 1, 100 % at f; cells sure 5–7 %, 3–5 %, 4–6 %, then 98–100 %. A puzzle is completed in ONE iteration and the confidence arrives with it. The aggregate "sure" curve (24 → 46 → 74 → 86 → 94 %) tracks the share of puzzles solved (18 → 44 → 73 → 86 → 94 %).

**3. Independent confirmation, argmax only** (`runs/analysis/repair_radius_20260917/*_A.npz`, 512 puzzles, up to 64 iterations, the fixed start; six models): for puzzles first solved at f = 2, 3, 4 the share of empty cells wrong at f − 1 has medians 23–31 %, 23–39 %, 28–43 %; puzzles never solved read 55–60 % wrong at iteration 1 and 52–57 % at 64. Puzzles solved by iteration 1 / 2 / 4: 16 / 45 / 73 % (width 192), 15 / 51 / 69 % (EqR), 25 / 67 / 81 % (width 384), 23 / 56 / 75 % (TRM reproduced).

**What it means.** The draft's smooth repair curves are population averages over puzzles that complete at different iterations. Per puzzle: a first guess about a third wrong, held at low confidence, then one iteration (21 applications of the block) that completes the grid. What happens inside that iteration is not measured here (the why-lens's per-cycle rows are population averages too). The readout gives no evidence of cell-by-cell fixing BETWEEN iterations; it does give evidence of a puzzle-level transition onto a state that is then kept (0 of 422,786 lost between 16 and 64 iterations on each headline seed).

**Owed before any of this is a claim:** A1 (the corpus under the training normalization) registers the aligned read with predictions (at f − 1 a median of at least 20 % of empty cells wrong; sure share under 15 % at f − 1 and over 90 % at f; calibration within 0.06), adds a per-outer-cycle read of the completing iteration, and moves these scripts into `tools/lens_commit_validity.py` with selftests.
