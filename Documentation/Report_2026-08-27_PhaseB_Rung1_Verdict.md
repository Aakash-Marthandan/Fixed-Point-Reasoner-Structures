## 2. The findings — five scale flips/extensions

### 2.1 The funnel changed owners: at width, the deep map holds the wide funnel
At d16 the T6@20k map owned breadth (S5 68.6 @128) and every T12 funnel was narrow (25.7–35.8). At d64 the FPA-T12 funnel reads **66.16 @128 / 77.3 @256** — the T12-class funnel roughly **doubled** with width — while the T6 recipe degraded (§2.2). BREADTH-FLAT is therefore a frontier statement (best-d64 ≈ best-d16), concealing an ownership inversion underneath. Spectroscopy (same estimator both scales): B2-d64's per-draw hit-rate is **~4–5× the d16 cells on every octile** with the flattest measured slope (−0.53 vs S5-d16 −0.67, d16-T12 class −0.83…−1.30) — **width raises the whole funnel and flattens its difficulty decay** (the holographic-capacity question, answered in the affirmative for deep maps). The vote@k curve is unsaturated (+9.7 pp on the last doubling, mirroring d16's shape). And the funnel **grows tail-first with training**: the mid ckpt (25k) already had the easy-octile rate (2.61 vs 2.48 %/draw) but no measurable hard tail (slope −4.16); by 50k the tail is uniform 0.03–0.05 %/draw across all hard octiles. Cold and funnel **co-grew** mid→final (21.83→25.27, 57.31→66.16) — at d16 they dissociated; at d64, on the anchored recipe, they move together.

### 2.2 H-45 at width: the collapse accelerates — 20k steps now suffice — and the price prevents it
The S5 recipe (plain T6@20k), stable at d16, **never forms a stable equilibrium at d64**: retfm and ret_sched are broken from the first monitor (B4 retfm .48/.75/.20/.38 across 5k–20k; B4s1 ret_sched down to .125), λ_joint > 1 at every monitor, val-selection lands on step 5000 (the selection itself sees the degradation). The wave-2 law said collapse comes with budget **or capacity**; the capacity axis is now measured: d16@20k stable → d32@20k collapsed → d64@20k collapsed, and faster than the 5k monitor cadence resolves (both precursors pre-5k; the d16 λ-precedes-retfm ordering is unresolvable here — cadence note for rung 2). **The clean stability contrast at width: B5 (priced T6, same steps/T/width) held retfm 1.00 throughout with λ contractive from the start.** The single toggle between broken and stable T6 at d64 is the toll — the price-as-contractivity-regulariser law (H-44's mechanism) extends to width, now protecting a recipe that plain training cannot hold at all.

### 2.3 The priced funnel opens with width mid-training, then the budget spends it away (new: H-47)
G-B3's letter (NARROW) reads from the vb screen. The mid screen flips the interpretation: **B3 at 25k had a wide funnel (71.3 @256 ≈ B2's 70.7) and lost it by 50k (27.0)** — and the endpoint k-curve at 50k is *flat* (24.6→27.0 across k=16→256): late training didn't merely narrow the funnel, it **condensed the draw distribution** (multi-init endpoints collapse to near-deterministic outcomes). The throat barely moved across the same window (1039→967 nats, −7%) while cold doubled (9.4→19.1 %): the reorganization happened at ~constant information budget. Reading: **at a fixed priced budget, cold-optimality and draw-diversity compete for the same nats** — the fitting term spends the ~1k-nat allowance on cold precision and the diversity that breadth needs is priced out. This unifies d16-priced-narrow (budget always binding at 1.0–1.6k nats) with the d64 mid-training window (code still forming → transient diversity). Registered as **H-47** with its test: β-ladder on the priced arm at width (does a larger budget let the funnel survive late training?) + screens on ≥3 checkpoints (the funnel-vs-training curve).

### 2.4 H-46 at width: cold-wander is d16-scoped; drift re-expresses in funnel space; RI/NI's fragility hardens into divergence
The d16 pathology was cold-space chaos (15.3 pp seed spreads, val-selection landing mid-training). At d64 **val-selected ≈ final on every healthy arm** (cold drifts ≤ 0.06 pp except collapsing B4's 2.50) — the cold-wander attenuation prediction was right; the finite-size reading of H-46 holds. What the rule's letter caught instead is **funnel drift** (6.6–44.3 pp) — newly instrumented at this rung — whose sign structure matters: B1/B2/B4s1/B5 funnels *grew* mid→final (healthy training direction), only B3 collapsed (§2.3). Meanwhile the d16 phase-boundary fragility of RI/NI (A2 vs A2s1's settle-or-not) hardened at d64 into an actual **NaN divergence at the registered lr** (B1, step 24.3k); at the labeled half-lr it reproduces the d16 A2 phenomenology exactly — long semi-contractive transient (retfm .56–.69 through 20k, λ_joint spikes to 10) then a settle to retfm 1.00 at 25–30k with η → 0.995 (the NI replacement-dynamics endpoint extends to d64). FPA (B2) holds retfm 1.00 from the first monitor with the final-map y-mode λ deeply contractive (.15–.21) — the anchor does at d64 exactly what it was built for.

### 2.5 The information laws extend across domain and scale
**Law 1/S1:** the priced throat on Sudoku is a task-set constant across 12× params — B3 967 / B5 985 nats at d64 vs 1.0–1.6k at d16, UV-concentrated profile intact (probe median I_s [1157, 36, 29, 17, 0] — the "third profile" replicates at width). **S2:** free flux inflates ~4× with width (plain arms 0.8–1.6M stream + 6.5–16.6M attention nats vs d16's 0.2–0.4M + 1.4–5.9M) — unpriced capacity spends its width on flux; priced capacity doesn't. Compression ≈ **1200×** with the stability dividend of §2.2. **Fisher-from-Adam-v** (descriptive, first use): curvature mass is extremely concentrated (participation ratio ~1e-4); on the stable deep arm the Fisher mass **migrates from the equilibrium-dynamics block to the readout/decoder** as training proceeds (B2: eq 70 %→37 %, readout 2 %→25 % — dynamics form first, then decoding refines); the collapsing arm stays eq-dominated with *falling* total curvature (B4: no consolidation — the collapse visible in metric space); the priced arm's curvature lives in the code blocks (dec/gate/enc ≈ 68 %), not the dynamics — under the toll, learning is a coding problem.

What IS open: whether 25k is the funnel's peak or its shoulder (no other screened ckpts) — rung-2's multi-ckpt screens answer it.
- *Statistics:* one seed pair total (B4); every cross-arm cold contrast is n=1 vs n=1 under a measured 1.5–7 pp noise scale — the flips claimed here ride instrument-level data (retfm n=512, funnels n=512/20k with tight CIs, trajectories), not cold contrasts.
