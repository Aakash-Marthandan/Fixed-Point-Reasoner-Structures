## 1. The build: what the DEC-ARC is, what exists, what is new

**The design, derived from the laws (freethink A-4, sharpened by the ARC pass).**

1. **The state:** ten color fields on the canvas, z_H / z_L of shape (F = 10, S = 900 or 1024, w), every parameter shared over the field axis, so a permutation of the colors permutes the state and the logits exactly (S10; the background is one of the ten and is distinguished only through the role embedding, which is what leaves S9 on the non-background colors physical). Exact color symmetry replaces the field's colour-permutation augmentation, as the S9 state replaced the digit orbit on Sudoku (−65 pp without it there; Law 6). D4 stays an augmentation (the corpus's orbit expansion; the eight views at evaluation are the consistency vote of §2).
2. **The role embedding per (field, cell):** the INPUT grid's color at this cell is this field / another field / the cell is outside the input's size — the Sudoku "given" roles generalized; the input is context, not a partial answer, so no cell is clamped.
3. **The readout per cell:** ten color logits from the field tokens (one shared vector per field) plus a VOID logit from the S10-invariant cell mean; the output size is the crop of the non-void region (the field's crop rule). The halting head reads the field-and-cell mean as on Sudoku; the commit head (C6's, τ ≥ 1 = inert, bit-identical by test) rides as the abstention instrument.
4. **The conditioning:** the demonstration pairs in the same ten-field representation, run through the shared block and pooled per field over cells and pairs into (F, w) task tokens added to every cell's field token — color-specific rules survive exactness because the tokens permute with the colors — plus one invariant token for the void decision. NO puzzle identity (the memorization channel measured at 26 pp on the field's model) and NO test-time fit (the natives' arm-A fit stays their protocol column, labeled D7; the DEC-ARC is read cold, with draws, and with the dihedral vote).
5. **The token mixer:** attention over the S cells per field (shared over fields; RoPE-2D or a learned position table, S10-safe), because the DEC's token-mixing SwiGLU over S = 81 cells does not scale to S = 900 (that is why the field's ARC model is TRM-Attention). The field coupling (the mean of the other nine fields at the cell) and the channel SwiGLU are the Sudoku DEC's.
6. **The loop and the objectives:** the champion loop (2 layers, H 3 × L 6, λ .05, β .01, 16 segments with ACT, deep supervision, stablemax, EMA .999, wd 1.0, lr 1e-4 after warmup) with RI σ 1 on the latent and FPA k 1 ε .2 frac .25 on the embedded output (the anchor rows = basin holding, which on ARC is the binding instrument), no NI. The FPA start at ε = 0 is the DEC's handed-truth RETENTION instrument (the field cells have no answer register, so retention is read by starting the loop at the embedded truth) and the ε ladder is its basin radius — the two ARC-era instruments come back on the DEC through the mechanism that trains for them.
7. **Width and compute (the MAC gate; `tools/mac_count.py`, 2026-09-10):** MEASURED per outer step per example (XLA's cost analysis of the evaluator's own step map): the Sudoku DEC-w192 14.82 GMAC, the DEC-w384 49.95, the set-attention arm 53.09, the field's ported TRM-MLP 12.87 (so the w192 is 1.15× the field's Sudoku arithmetic, the w384 3.9×), the native rg cell at d64 on the ARC canvas 3.44. ANALYTIC for the DEC-ARC design (per token per block: attention 4w² + 2Sw, SwiGLU 12w², coupling 2w²; 9,000 tokens; 2 layers × (H 3 × L 6 + 3) block passes per segment; the same formula gives the field's ARC-1 TRM-Attention 141 GMAC per segment at hidden 512 over 916 tokens): w128 199 GMAC per segment = 1.41× the field's ARC cell, w160 283 = 2.0×, w192 381 = 2.7×, w256 620 = 4.4×; parameters ≈ 18w² per block × 2 ≈ 0.6 M (w128) / 0.9 M (w160) / 1.3 M (w192) against the field's 6.83 M non-embedding + the 449 M-entry identity table. **Base width w160** (the 2× point, ≈ 0.9 M parameters, no table; w192 is 2.7× and w128 the 1.4× floor); the pilot's pace decides whether 160 holds or 128 is taken (§5). HBM (inferred from the champion's 729-token examples at batch 768 on 4 chips with remat): 9,000-token examples → batch ≈ 64–96 per host.
8. **The data and the selection:** the pretrain13 corpus (re-arc 20 per family + the ARC training split minus dev-30 + ConceptARC minus val-hard, the family holdout for the rg gate, orbit 4), so the cell is the only change against the natives; the 2k monitor = per-output exact on the val slice with dev-30 as the second key, earliest tie, the raw monitor as the tie-breaker; the onset instrument = the monitor's plateau end; 30k steps with the registered extension (+10k once if the selected grid is in the last 4k).

**What exists (reused, not rewritten):** `qhrrn2.dec_cell` (the field-shared block stack, the coupling, the role embedding, the halting mean, embed_answer for the FPA anchor, the commit head), `qhrrn2.trm_cell` (the loop, RI, the segment loop with ACT and deep supervision), `tools/pretrain.py`'s field loop and FPA rows, the 2k monitor with earliest-tie selection, the census, `tools/arc_suite.py`'s draws / selectors / retention / dynamics rows, `qhrrn2.episodic`'s corpus and the rg/rt gates, the chain/harness pattern of the champion night, the frozen-analyzer pattern with a selftest.

**What is new (the build list, ≈ 2 days):** B1 `dec_cell` generalized: F as a config, the attention token mixer, the input-role embedding, the void readout, the support encoder (the same block over the pairs, pooled per field); B2 the trainer's ARC field loop: episodic batches → ten-field inputs and targets (void as a target class), the size-aware exactness, the monitor on the val slice + dev-30, the FPA rows on the embedded output, the family/orbit plumbing; the Sudoku-only asserts lifted for `--cell dec --arc`; B3 the evaluator: cold traces with per-step exactness, latent draws (k), the FPA-start retention and ladder, the dihedral vote (8 views; any-view, count vote, the consistency row), the color-permutation flip test, C1/C2/C3 rows, the halting and commit heads, rg / rt / val-hard / dev-30 and the ARC-AGI-1 public evaluation (400 tasks: cold pass@1, k 8 draws, the vote's pass@2) — one tool, `tools/eval_decarc.py`, sharing `arc_suite.py`'s record schema so `lens_arc_suite_read.py` reads both; B4 `tools/analyze_decarc.py --selftest` with the registered rules and predictions (§4), the onset definition, the INTEGRITY gates (one grid per (arm, vsel), counts, the color-flip floor); B5 `tools/chain_decarc.sh` + `tools/harness_decarc.sh` (idempotent GCS markers, the 5-min live bank, the extension rule, the remat retry, the rider queue) with every negative scenario asserting its staged failure; B6 tests: exact S10 equivariance of the whole forward (a color permutation of input, supports and target permutes the logits exactly), τ ≥ 1 bit-identity of the commit head, the void readout's invariance, the FPA ε = 0 start reproducing the target, CPU smokes of the real trainer at the real width with every registry flag, the evaluator's rows on real tasks, `analyze_decarc.py` on no data exiting clean, the harness's exact output names.

## 4. Prediction stubs (bands locked at registration after the pilot; credences then)

- **P1 exactness:** the color-permutation flip rate on unseen inputs reads the numerics floor (≤ the compute-route floor) on every DEC-ARC arm; the natives' rate (G1) is the comparison.
- **P2 parity gate:** val-hard per-output cold ≥ the native d64 twin's 7.6 % on the identical 144 queries (the DEC-ARC must not be worse than the cell it replaces); band after the pilot.
- **P3 the depth law on one cell across domains:** exact-by-step on the DEC-ARC — flat from step 2 (the domain owns depth) or rising (the cell owns it); either reading is a §6 sentence; regressions counted at exact pairing.
- **P4 the selector law's value:** residual AUC ≥ .85 on D0/D1 AND oracle@32 − cold > 0 (coverage exists under RI + FPA where Dri condensed); D2's AUC below D0/D1's by the Sudoku margin.
- **P5 basin existence:** FPA-start retention at ε 0 ≥ .9 on solved pairs and > the natives' 19–28 % on ALL pairs (the anchors train basin holding); the oracle conditions on retention (the ARC era's 31×) — if retention rises without the oracle, the inventory is there and the search is not.
- **P6 the failure texture:** converged-wrong of converged ≥ .9 with 1 flip per cell (ARC's texture survives the cell) — or churn appears (the cell's texture ports); commitment at step 1 read against 78 % (natives) and 93–96 % (the Sudoku DEC).
- **P7 the dihedral vote's worth:** any-view − identity and the count-vote's collection; the consistency row (distinct answers solved vs failed) reproducing the field's .76 / .001 separation.
- **P8 the public number:** ARC-AGI-1 evaluation pass@2 with the 8-view vote, no identity, no fit — reported with its columns, whatever it lands; the field's 43.0 (7 M + table + 1,000 views) and FPRM's 47.5 (no TTT, no aug) beside it.
- **P9 the control:** N0 reproduces the d96 rung's registered bands (rg-96 ≥ 23.5, N 42–50, I < 499) or the rung's kill; the throat / profile rows scale-match D6.

## 9. Registration draft (2026-09-10 evening; locked at the registration commit after the adversarial review; the pilot fills the two [PILOT] slots)

**Amendments to §1 from the build (audited against the code, not the sketch):**
- **§1.4 conditioning → the natives' protocol, made colour-indexed.** The support encoder is NOT built for this shot. The DEC-ARC conditions on a per-task code exactly as the natives do — a learned row per training task, the deployed arm-A fit of the code on a task's support pairs at test time (600 steps; `eval_dev30._fit`, never re-implemented) — but the code is (F = 10, d_task = 32) per colour, projected per field by one shared matrix, so the joint action of a colour permutation on the input and on the code's field axis is an exact symmetry of the map (`tests/test_decarc.py::test_decarc_exact_s10`). Consequence: the cell is the ONLY variable against the natives (same corpus, same conditioning protocol, same fit), and every native evaluation tool runs on the DEC-ARC unchanged (`model.forward_fields` takes the code as `task_vec`; the size is decoded from the VOID readout through `model.decode_size`, the rg cells' decode bit-exact). The identity-free / fit-free support encoder is paper 2's object. D7 is labeled identically for both cells.
- **§1.7 width w160** (the measured MAC gate: 2.0× the field's ARC-1 cell per segment, ≈ 0.9 M parameters with the 1,024-cell position table).
- **§1.6 the FPA rows on ARC** corrupt inside the OUTPUT region uniformly over the ten colours and anchor through the input's role table (void = the "outside" role), so the eps = 0 start is the handed-truth retention instrument and the eps ladder the basin radius; the loss on the DEC-ARC averages the output region and the VOID region separately with `w_void` .5 (the size is a target of the VOID readout).
- **The 2k monitor** = the cold rollout at T = 16 on the held-out queries of `n_val` = 96 training tasks with their trained code rows (per-output exact; the EMA weights select, the raw monitor second, earliest tie); the natives' N0 selects on its 2k val rows (`select_ckpt --row val --key val_frac --tie earliest`). ## 10. Adversarial review and pre-mortem of the build (2026-09-10 evening; what the audit changed, what it could not verify, what would fool us)

**What the audit changed before the pod (each a defect the reading caught, fixed and re-tested):**
1. **The probe traces ignored the field cells' pinned dampings.** `probe_e1e3.trace`, `arc_suite.trace_conf` and the new orbit tool computed eta / eta_z from sigmoid(params) — right for the rg cells (learned, floor 0), wrong for any field cell (eta_z pinned at 1.0): the DEC-ARC's trace damped its carried latent by half and its preds diverged from `train.predict`. All three now read `model.eq_etas` (the rg cells bit-exact; `tests/test_decarc.py::test_decarc_deployed_fit_and_probe_trace`, `tests/test_probe_e1e3.py` green).
2. **The task-code table would not have trained under the field regime.** The natives train their code table at the model's lr 1e-3; the DEC-ARC's field regime is lr 1e-4 with wd 1.0, and a code row is touched only ≈ B × steps / n_tasks ≈ 1,000 times — the rows would stay near their 0.1-normal init and decay. TRM's ARC recipe trains its puzzle embeddings at lr 1e-2 with decay .1 for exactly this reason. `tools/pretrain.py --table-lr 1e-2 --table-wd 0.1` (optax.multi_transform over the two top-level keys; the default path byte-identical) is in the registry.

4. **The `--cell` choice list, the analyzer's record paths and the stub's resume** — each caught by a smoke or the harness, each fixed.

**What would fool us (pre-mortem), and the guard on each:**

- *The 2k monitor selects a memorizer* (its queries are held-out queries of TRAINED tasks, not unseen tasks): the val-hard rows are the few-shot instrument; a MEMORIZED letter with a PARITY letter is the reading that says the monitor and the target disagree — the report separates them.

- *A preemption mid-round loses an arm's evals*: every row is banked by marker and re-run only if missing (S9); the pretrain resumes from the 5-min live bank (S11).
