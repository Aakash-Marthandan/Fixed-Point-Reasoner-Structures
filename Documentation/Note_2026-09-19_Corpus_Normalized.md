The segment code is identical line for line (`dec_cell.segment`); the two are different compiled kernels, and an unsolved puzzle's trajectory amplifies round-off. This is the compute-route floor the paper already reports, seen at the cell level.
**The new gate:** the probe must equal the evaluator on more than 99.9 % of the empty cells at iteration 1 AND on the puzzles the evaluator has solved through iteration 4; the per-iteration agreement, the agreement on solved puzzles and the two solved counts at 16 are recorded in each file's metadata and printed in the report. Part C's reads are statistical and within the probe's own trajectories; they do not require trajectory identity with the evaluator.
**For the paper:** a sentence in the limitations. Which hard puzzles a model solves at a given depth depends on the compute route (7 of 128 rating-stratified puzzles here); the rates do not (119 against 122).
