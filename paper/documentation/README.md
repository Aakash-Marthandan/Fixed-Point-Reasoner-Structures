# paper/documentation — the writing documentation

The files that keep the paper in one voice, the PI's, and one vocabulary. Read in this order before drafting or revising any section.

| file | what it is | when to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `VOICE_AND_STYLE.md` | the voice, style and tone markers extracted from the PI's dissertation (sentence-, paragraph- and section-level; mathematical writing; what the voice never does; the register shift to a conference paper; calibration sentences) | while drafting every paragraph; the PI's own edits update §7 |
| `PAPER_BLUEPRINT.md` | what the paper must teach (the physics and the RG connection operationally, the instruments as implementable definitions, the laws with scope and evidence), the section plan with page budgets, the RG correspondence table's discipline, the tables/figures discipline, the EqR-like presentation made explicit, the workflow | before drafting a section; when deciding what goes in the main text vs the appendix |
| `TERMINOLOGY.md` | the fixed vocabulary (objects, measurements, comparators) with one definition each and the synonyms that are deleted on sight; the house rules on numbers and names | whenever a term is introduced or a number is written |
| `STYLE_CHECKLIST.md` | the pre-commit checklist per paragraph, number, definition/instrument/law, section and paper; the LaTeX conventions | before any section is committed or sent to the professor |
| `LITERATURE_REVIEW.md` | the protocol-labeled comparator table for Sudoku-Extreme (training set, steps, restarts, selection, search, test set), our honest position per column, the mechanistic neighbors and how to cite them, what the review changes in the paper, the owed measurements | before writing any comparison, the results table, related work or limitations |
| `CONCURRENT_GAPS.md` | per concurrent paper: the gap its authors state (quoted), what our instruments and laws read on it, the confidence label, the measurement that would settle it, the sentence the paper can carry | when writing §5, §7 and §8 |
| `ABSTRACT_DRAFTS.md` | the abstract's version log with sources and the reason for each change | when revising the abstract |
| `claims.tex` | the claim ledger (not compiled) the abstract and introduction draw from | when deciding what a section may claim |

Source of the voice analysis: `/Users/aakash/Projects/Second Brain/Context dump/PhD_Dissertation.pdf` (Aakash Marthandan, *Algebraic Structures of Entanglement: From Local Unitary Stabilizers to Perturbative Modular Flow*, CUNY 2026), sampled across the abstract, chapter 1 (§1.1–1.4), chapter 2 (§2.1–2.2, §2.6), chapter 3 (§3.1–3.2), chapter 4 (§4.1–4.6) and chapter 5 (§5.1–5.4). The science the paper reports comes from the repository's ledger and analysis reports, never from memory.
